# Listing

## Top Ten Study Spots in Osaka

My theme is places to actually get work done around Osaka, ranked by how often I end up going back.
Campus libraries first, then the free public options, then the paid ones for when everything else is full.

1. **Osaka University Main Library (Toyonaka)** - the obvious one. Big, quiet, and the upper floors stay empty even in exam season.
2. **Life Sciences Library (Suita)** - closest to the IST buildings, so it wins on walking distance alone. Good for a gap between lectures.
3. **Minoh Library** - worth the trip if you want a floor to yourself. Smallest of the three, almost never crowded.
4. **Osaka Prefectural Central Library (Aramoto)** - free, huge, and the study desks are separated, which is the part that matters.
5. **Osaka City Central Library (Nishinagahori)** - same idea, closer to the city centre. Handy if you are already in Namba for something else.
6. **Toyonaka City Library, Senri-Chuo branch** - two stops down the monorail from Senri-Chuo, so it is easy to reach from Suita campus.
7. **Kaikatsu Club (private booths)** - not free, but you get a door, a power socket, and free drinks. The one I use when I need to finish something at 2am.
8. **Komeda's Coffee** - the booths are wide and nobody rushes you out. Cheapest way to buy three hours of desk space.
9. **Grand Front Osaka, Umeda** - open seating on the lower floors. Noisy, so this is for reading and email, not for anything that needs thinking.
10. **Suita campus cafeteria, off-peak** - free, warm, and completely dead between 14:00 and 17:00. Terrible from 12:00 to 13:00.

### Notes on the list

The ranking is personal and the top three are only top three because I am on the Suita campus.
Anyone based in Toyonaka would probably reorder them.
